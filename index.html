How We Work

5-Stage Turnkey Engineering Lifecycle

Structured project management designed to eliminate commissioning surprises and protect your production schedule.

  1. 01

    Site Audit & FDS Consultation

    We audit your existing control panels, network health, and process bottlenecks to author a clear Functional Design Specification (FDS).

  2. 02

    Electrical CAD & Panel Fabrication

    Detailed electrical schematic drafting, component sourcing, and neat industrial control panel assembly built to international standards.

  3. 03

    PLC/SCADA Code & Simulation FAT

    Modular PLC, drive, HMI, and IIoT software development followed by rigorous Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) before shipping to site.

  4. 04

    Site Installation, Cutover & SAT

    Planned shutdown cutover, field wiring verification, loop tuning, network validation, and full Site Acceptance Testing (SAT).

  5. 05

    Training, As-Built Docs & 24/7 Support

    Complete as-built documentation, customized operator/maintenance training, and scheduled or on-call lifecycle support.
